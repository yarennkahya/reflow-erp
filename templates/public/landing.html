The Problem

Business operations shouldn't live
in separate systems.

When CRM, inventory, finance, and production don't share a common operational context, visibility disappears and decisions are made blind.

Without a connected ERP
Separate tools for inventory, sales, and finance — no shared context
Manual data transfers between spreadsheets and isolated apps
Stockouts and overstock discovered too late — no automated detection
Finance team reconciling data from 5 different sources every month
No traceability — where did that stock go? who placed that order?
Decisions made from partial, stale information
→
With one connected system
Every module shares a single operational context and data layer
Events propagate automatically — a sale updates inventory in real time
Background automation detects critical stock and triggers purchasing
Finance data is generated from operational events — no reconciliation
Full traceability — every lot, movement, order, and payment is logged
AI layer provides context-aware decision support on real ERP data

Operations / Inventory
Complete stock visibility, lot by lot.
Manage products, lots, warehouses, and stock movements through a ledger-based approach. Every movement is a recorded event — nothing is simply "changed." This gives full traceability from arrival to consumption.

Freshness windows are tracked per lot. Automated alerts detect lots approaching expiry before they become a problem. Critical stock levels trigger purchasing workflows.

Purchasing Production Sales AI Assistant
Operations / Purchasing
From supplier to stock — fully tracked.
Manage suppliers and their product catalogs. Create, track, and advance purchase orders through the full lifecycle: Draft → Sent → Confirmed → Received. Goods receipt creates new inventory lots automatically through StockMovement records.

Low stock detection can trigger draft purchase orders automatically via background tasks — reducing manual monitoring.
Connects with
Inventory Notifications Finance
Operations / Production
Recipe-driven, BOM-based production control.
Define recipes (bills of materials) that specify which raw materials are consumed and in what ratios. Create production batches that automatically record material consumption as stock OUT movements and finished goods as stock IN.

Quality checks are attached to each batch. Passed lots enter regular inventory. Failed lots are handled separately. The entire production chain is traceable.
Connects with
Inventory Sales AI Assistant
Commerce / Sales
From order to fulfillment — with full traceability.
Manage wholesale and retail customers, their orders, and order items. Fulfillment creates stock OUT movements automatically. Return requests flow through an approval workflow. Demand forecasting provides visibility into future needs.

Every sales event connects back to inventory, finance, and CRM — no isolated records.
Connects with
Inventory Finance CRM
Commerce / CRM
Pipeline visibility from prospect to payment.
Track sales opportunities through configurable pipeline stages: New → In Discussion → Proposal → Negotiation → Won / Lost. Won opportunities connect directly to order creation, preserving the full customer journey in one system.

Customer activity logs, meetings, and communication history stay attached to the customer record — not scattered across emails.

Finance
Financial clarity from operational data.
Invoices, payments, revenue tracking, and profitability reports are generated directly from operational events — not from separate accounting data entry. Revenue comes from fulfilled orders. Costs come from purchase orders.

Partial payments, payment methods, and invoice status are tracked. Financial summaries are always derived from source operational records.

People / HR
Workforce management in the same system.
Departments, positions, employees, leave requests, and recruitment pipelines — all managed within the ERP rather than in a separate HR tool. Leave requests flow through approval workflows. Job openings manage applications from applied to hired.

HR data is connected to meetings, operational activities, and AI insights — your people data lives alongside your operational data.

Intelligence / AI Assistant
Intelligence connected to operational context.
The AI layer isn't a generic chatbot attached to the side of the system. It's connected to ERP data through RAG (semantic retrieval) and controlled function calling. Controlled AI Access
The AI does not have unrestricted access to the database. It operates through a defined set of authorized functions — it can read operational data and provide insights, but every action is permission-gated. This is an enterprise architectural choice, not a limitation.

Automation

The system monitors
so you don't have to.

Background tasks run continuously on the operational data — detecting critical states, generating notifications, and triggering workflows automatically.

Critical Stock Detection
Nightly scan compares stock levels against reorder thresholds. Products below the minimum automatically generate draft purchase orders.
Freshness Monitoring
Lot freshness status is evaluated continuously. Aging lots are flagged as priority sale or waste, with notifications generated automatically.
Defective Return Scanning
Pending return requests with quality issues are scanned on schedule. Approved returns update inventory through proper StockMovement records.
Automated Notifications
Operational events generate targeted notifications. Leave approvals, stock alerts, order updates — delivered to the right users in real time.
Scheduled Background Tasks
Celery Beat runs recurring tasks on a schedule — no manual triggers required. Tasks are asynchronous and do not block the application.
Audit Trail
Every significant action is logged — who changed what and when. Full traceability for every inventory movement, order status change, and workflow transition.
